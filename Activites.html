Latest Articles From Activities

Ambedkar Jayanthi

On 14thApril – Ambedkar Jayanthi was celebrated in the school assembly. Students of Grade 9 and 10 were present on the occasion.

School reopened for the academic year 2025-26

On 26th May – School reopened for the academic year 2025-26 with a total strength of 1495 students with 776 girls and 719 boys including the new admission. Total Teaching Staff comprises of 68 members with 51 lady teachers and 17 gents teachers and non-teaching staff with 23 ladies and 15 gents. Grade 1 kids were received by the teachers with a pencil as a small token of love for every student.

World Environment Day

On 5th June – World Environment Day was celebrated in the school assembly. Chief Guest was Mr B V Gundappa, An Environmentalist, Lecturer in Biology, C S Pura, Gubbi Taluk. He gave a talk on the conservation of environment. Hon’ble KVS office bearers were present on the occasion.

Forthcoming Diamond Jubilee Celebration

On 19th June – On account of forthcoming Diamond Jubilee Celebration of Kapataru Vidya Samsthe, Stone – laying ceremony for the new building of the school was held. Mr. Shadakshari, MLA, Tiptur was the Chief Guest. All the KVS Management members were present on the occasion.

International Yoga Day

On 21st June – International Yoga Day & World Music Day was celebrated in the school assembly. Chief guests were Smt. Chennambika, Sri Kantharaju & Smt Lakshmi, Vivekananda Yoga Training School, Tiptur.
Various Asanas of Yoga were demonstrated by Mr. Rakhesh, Physical Educator and many students of our school. Carnatic Classical music singer Mrs. Kavyasudha, Principal Scientist in a Pharmacuetical Company was the Chief Guest for the Music Day Celebration.
On the same day, Inter-house Song Competition for middle school level was conducted. Theme given was -The Environment. The 1 st place was bagged by Utkarsh House. 2 nd and 3 rd prizes were secured by Samyak and Vriddhi House respectively.

Awareness on Child Safety

On 23rd June:- An awareness programme on Child Safety Education was conducted. The students from Grade 1 to 5 were shown the videos on Good Touch and Bad Touch.
On the same day, an orientation programme was held on POCSO Act by the principal for the School Bus-Drivers and all the attenders as done every year.

Doctors’ day

On 1stJuly – Doctors’ day was celebrated in the school assembly. Dr Vivechan, a renowned physician in Tiptur was the Chief Guest. The doctor addressed the gathering on right ways to sustain and improve one’s health condition.

Fire Safety Training

On 9th July – Fire Extinguishing Demo was initiated by Karnataka State Fire & Emergency Services in association with Disaster Management Committee, KCS in the Kalpataru Grounds. The High School students performed a mock drill for an emergency evacuation in case of any unforeseen disaster.

Zonal Level tournaments

From 4th – 10 th Aug The school has been encouraging the students to represent and participate in various Zonal Level tournaments held outside Tiptur.
Rishika Jain, Jeevan M U from 9 th Std and Gowrav from 8 th Std have represented our school and won the Bronze Medals in the Taekwondo CBSE Zonal Competition held in Pune.
The School Volley Team escorted by Mr. Siddegowda, Physical Education Teacher participated in the Volley-Ball Tournament held in Davangere.
The School Kho – Kho Team escorted by Mr. Chandrashekar, Mr. Rakhesh and Mrs. Lathamani had been to Raichur for the tournament. Boys team made through the Quarter-Finals.

The Grand Finale of KCS Debate Championship 2025-26 (Season 9)

On 13th Aug –The Grand Finale of KCS Debate Championship 2025-26 (Season 9) was held in the KCS premises.
The judges were Mrs. Vinutha Tejpal HOD, English Dept PAAC. Mr. Sachin Govind Naddoni, HOD, Chemistry Dept, Kalpataru P U College.
The finalist teams, The Kalpataru Argument Architects team led by Monika S S emerged as the Champions. Kalpataru El Dialectos team led by NiharikaKamaraj became the First Runner-up and KalpataruVox Suprema team led by Varsha K secured the Second Runner-up trophy.
The Office Bearers of Kalpataru Vidya Samsthe Sri T S Shivaprasad, The Treasurer, KVS and Sri M R Sangamesh, The Secretary, KVS and Smt. Devika B Swamy, The Principal, KCS witnessed the competition and appreciated the participants.
The High School students were present to encourage the participants.
