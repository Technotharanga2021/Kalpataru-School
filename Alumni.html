Alumni Association – Kalpataru Central School

The Alumni Association of Kalpataru Central School is a proud community that connects our former students who continue to make remarkable achievements in various fields. Our alumni have excelled in prestigious domains such as Medicine, Engineering, and the Armed Forces, bringing immense pride and inspiration to their alma mater.
The association serves as a bridge between the past and present, fostering meaningful interaction among alumni, students, and teachers. Through mentoring sessions, motivational talks, and collaborative initiatives, our alumni guide current students toward academic and personal excellence.
At Kalpataru Central School, we take great pride in the accomplishments of our alumni, who embody the school’s core values of discipline, dedication, and service. Their achievements stand as a testament to the holistic education and strong foundation provided by the institution.
The Alumni Association continues to nurture this enduring bond, celebrating the success stories of our graduates and inspiring future generations to soar higher in every walk of life.

Pranav Bellur - Flying Officer - The Indian Air Force

They say that a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.
For me, that first step has always been my school for which I’m eternally grateful.
I take immense pride to have been part of this esteemed institution .
The discipline, knowledge and confidence that I gained here has continued to guide me in every step of life.
My heartfelt gratitude to all my teachers for imparting such good knowledge and values which is the core of any individual.

Harshith - COMMERCIAL PILOT - Avconjet - South Africa

Flying on the blue horizon in the Rainbow nation - South Africa’s Grand Central Airport, I was instructing my student to fly multi-engine aircraft on a bright sunny day. The weather was quite pleasant, and the sky embedded with some cottony clouds...
My mind was going through a wave of emotions and my visit to India. All of a sudden, the person that came to my mind was Mrs. Devika ma’am whom I had an interaction over the phone, during my visit to India last year December…
Devika Ma’am – Hello Harshith. How are you?
Me – I am fine, thank you. How are you Ma’am?
Devika Ma’am - A gap of so many years, you are now in my contact…
Me – Oh! Yes Ma’am…
Devika Ma’am – How have you been and what have you been up to?
Me – I am a Flight instructor and examiner in South Africa.
Devika Ma’am – Oh good! What made you go there and pursue this career?
Being in the first batch of CBSE at Kalpataru Central School, I had extremely good relations with the principal and teachers then. I learnt a lot at the school. It instilled confidence in me. I express gratitude to my school. I owe a lot to the school for offering me a strong foundational course.
Later, I did not want to pursue the usual course in engineering or medicine. My search went on to grab an alternate career option. I made up my mind to become a ‘COMMERCIAL PILOT’.
South Africa, being one of the best training providers for Pilots was my target. Travelled to Nelspruit for my PPL (Private Pilots License) and then made my way to Grand Central Airport, Johannesburg for my advanced training and CPL (Commercial Pilots License). Turned it up a notch with Instructors Rating and Examiners licenses.
After a tenure of about 7 years of Instruction, I am now with Avconjet Africa and have several aircraft ratings marked in my License with over 7000 hours of flying…
Next in my way is ‘Mission India.’ I will share my successful story next-
Always remember – Dream Big and Fly High !!!

Nischitha T Y -Sub Lieutenant - The Indian Navy

I am highly thankful to my alma mater, Kalpataru Central School - my nurturer, my haven, a place I called my own for a decade, my school.
As far as I am concerned, I can very humbly say, I am what I am today because of my schooling. I am now a part of one of the elite defence systems of our country - The Indian Navy. The credit goes to my school, a place where we were allowed to dream, think out of the box and given freedom to explore our interests.
I realised the true meaning of having a great formative education during the journey I took to become an officer. One has to go through a plethora of exams and attend what's called a SSB interview- which is a 5-day overall personality test to determine one's officer like quality.
My grip on my language, my analytical thinking, my ability to work in a group and express my ideas to a large audience with the utmost precision and conviction and well, the infinite amount of confidence I had during the gruesome interview -all these attributes helped me succeed.
Where did I develop all these qualities from? - My School. This was a result of the years spent in this elite institution, which moulded me to become a complete individual.
What a marvellous journey it was at Kalpataru Central School - transforming me from a tiny tot to a naughty and inquisitive child to a gregarious teen and finally passing out as a relatively mature adolescent.
Be it in terms of sports, music, art, culture, public speaking, debates, declamation, worldly knowledge or just plain old algebra- I have a working knowledge of all these things and for this I am forever indebted to the amazing teachers at this institute.
I take immense pleasure in paying rich tributes to Kalpataru Central School and Kalpataru Vidya Samsthe which is incidentally celebrating Diamond Jubilee this year.
