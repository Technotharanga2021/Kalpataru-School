August Month Activities

Interschool Science Seminar Competition
2nd August - Our students from Grade 10, Kumari Niharika Kamraj and Kumari Varsha K participated in the Interschool Science Seminar Competition organized by Kalpataru PU College Science Department and bagged 1st Prize and 2nd Prize respectively.
Zonal Level Tournaments
4th – 10th Aug – Students represented our school in various Zonal Level tournaments.
• Rishika Jain, Jeevan M U (Grade 9) and Gowrav (Grade 8) won Bronze in Taekwondo CBSE Zonal Competition (Pune).
• School Volleyball Team participated in the Davangere Tournament.
• School Kho-Kho Team reached Quarter-Finals in Raichur.
State-level Speech Competition
11th Aug – High School students represented our school in the State-level Speech Competition in Bengaluru initiated by Verbattle Foundation; 6 students qualified for Semi-Finals.
On the same day, in the Patriotic Songs Competition, Kumari Gana (Grade 10) won 1st prize and Kumari Bhuvaneshwari (Grade 4) won 2nd prize.
Arivina Payana – Value Based Session
12th Aug – Sri Nadamani Nalkoor held a value-based session “Arivina Payana” in our school, inspiring high school students with motivating songs and essential life lessons.
KCS Debate Championship 2025-26 (Season 9)
13th Aug – Grand Finale of KCS Debate Championship was held in KCS premises.
• Champions: Kalpataru Argument Architects (led by Monika S S)
• 1st Runner-up: Kalpataru El Dialectos (led by Niharika Kamaraj)
• 2nd Runner-up: Kalpataru Vox Suprema (led by Varsha K)
Independence Day Celebration
15th Aug – Celebrated with zeal in school and Kalpataru Grounds.
• 260 high school students bagged 1st prize in Taluk Level Patriotic Dance Competition.
• Boys Troop won 2nd prize in March-Past Competition.
Sri Krishna Janmashtami Celebration
16th Aug – Janmashtami was celebrated with devotion. Students performed cultural programs on the occasion.
Ganesha Idol-Making Competition
23rd Aug – A Ganesha Idol-Making Competition was held using natural clay under CCA activities for Ganesha festival.
Gowri-Ganesha Festival Celebration
25th Aug – Celebrated in the school assembly with joy. Vice President Mr. B S Umesh, Secretary Mr. M R Sangamesh, and Principal Smt. Devika B Swamy witnessed the celebration.
Taluk-level Dasara Sports Competition
27th Aug – Students won multiple medals at Taluk-level Dasara Sports Competition, Kalpataru Grounds, Tiptur.
• Golds: Sanjana Patel (Relay), Poshika T.S. (Relay, Kho-Kho), Raksha N.R. (200m, 400m, Relay), Manvitha G.M. (Relay)
• Silver/Bronze in various categories
• Kho-Kho Girls Team: Gold
• PE Teacher Mr. Rakhesh B S: Gold & Bronze medals
Athletics Competition
28th Aug – Students participated in Athletics Competition at CBSE Cluster Level in Davangere.
National Sports Day Celebration
29th Aug – Celebrated to commemorate Major Dhyanchand. Grade 10 students attended an awareness programme on “Entrepreneurship as a Career” at Kalpataru Institute of Technology.
