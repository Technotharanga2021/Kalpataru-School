August Month Activities

Interschool Science Seminar Competition

2 nd August - Our students from Grade 10, Kumari Niharika Kamraj and Kumari Varsha K participated in the Interschool Science Seminar Competition organized by Kalpataru PU College Science Department and consequently bagged 1 st Prize and 2 nd Prize respectively making the school proud of them.

Zonal Level tournaments

4 th – 10 th Aug The school has been encouraging the students to represent and participate in various Zonal Level tournaments held outside Tiptur.  Rishika Jain, Jeevan M U from 9 th Std and Gowrav from 8 th Std have represented our school and won the Bronze Medals in the Taekwondo CBSE Zonal Competition held in Pune.  The School Volley Team escorted by Mr. Siddegowda, Physical Education Teacher participated in the Volley-Ball Tournament held in Davangere.  The School Kho – Kho Team escorted by Mr. Chandrashekar, Mr. Rakhesh and Mrs. Lathamani had been to Raichur for the tournament. Boys team made through the Quarter-Finals.

State-level Speech Competition

11 th Aug – The selected High School students represented our school in the State-level Speech Competition in Bengaluru initiated by the Verbattle Foundation. 6 students qualified for the Semi-Finals. The Patriotic Songs Competition was organized by the BEO Office, Tiptur on the same day. Kumari Gana from 10 th std bagged the 1 st prize and Kumari Bhuvaneshwari from 4 th std bagged the 2 nd prize.

Arivina Payana A Value Based – Session by Sri Nadamani Nalkoor

12 th Aug – Arivina Payana A Value Based – Session by Sri Nadamani Nalkoor, was held in our school. The esteemed guest communicated the essential life values to high school students through his motivating songs.

The Grand Finale of KCS Debate Championship 2025-26 (Season 9)

13 th Aug –The Grand Finale of KCS Debate Championship 2025-26 (Season 9) was held in the KCS premises. The judges were Mrs. Vinutha Tejpal HOD, English Dept PAAC. Mr. Sachin Govind Naddoni, HOD, Chemistry Dept, Kalpataru P U College. The finalist teams, The Kalpataru Argument Architects team led by Monika S S emerged as the Champions. Kalpataru El Dialectos team led by NiharikaKamaraj became the First Runner-up and KalpataruVox Suprema team led by Varsha K secured the Second Runner-up trophy. The Office Bearers of Kalpataru Vidya Samsthe Sri T S Shivaprasad, The Treasurer, KVS and Sri M R Sangamesh, The Secretary, KVS and Smt. Devika B Swamy, The Principal, KCS witnessed the competition and appreciated the participants. The High School students were present to encourage the participants.se. 2 nd and 3 rd prizes were secured by Samyak and Vriddhi House respectively.

Independence Day celebration

15 th Aug - Independence Day was celebrated in the school and in the Kalpataru Grounds with lot of zeal and enthusiasm. About 260 high school students participated in the Taluk Level Patriotic Dance Competition at the Kalpataru Stadium & bagged the first prize. Our School Troop (Boys) bagged 2 nd prize in the March-Past Competition.

Sri Krishna Janmashtami celebration

16 th Aug – Sri Krishna Janmashtami was celebrated with lot of devotion in the school. The students gave various cultural programmes on the occasion.

Ganesha Idol - Making Competition

23 rd Aug - Ganesha Idol - Making Competition using natural clay under the CCA Competitions was conducted on account of Ganesha festival.

Gowri-Ganesha festival was celebration

25 th Aug - Gowri-Ganesha festival was celebrated in the school assembly spreading the joy and festivity. Mr. B S Umesh, Vice President and Mr. M R Sangamesh, Secretary, KVS along with Smt. Devika B Swamy, the principal were delighted to witness the celebration.

Taluk-level Dasara Sports Competition

27 th Aug – Our young athletes made us proud at the Taluk-level Dasara Sports Competition held at Kalpataru Grounds, Tiptur, by bagging multiple medals across events. Sanjana Patel – Gold (4×100m Relay), Silver (800m), Silver (400m) Poshika T.S. – Gold (4×100m Relay), Gold (Kho-Kho), Silver (200m), Silver (3000m) Raksha N.R. – Gold (200m), Gold (400m), Gold (4×100m Relay) Manvitha G.M. – Gold (4×100m Relay), Silver (1500m) Suhas – Bronze (800m), Bronze (400m) Kho-Kho Girls Team – Gold Our Physical Education teacher Mr. Rakhesh B S also bagged multiple medals mentioned as under: Javelin Throw: Bronze 4×100m Relay: Gold 4×400m Relay: Gold

Athletics Competition

28 th Aug - Our students participated in Athletics Competition held at CBSE Cluster Level held in Davanagere.

National Sports Day celebration

29 th Aug - National Sports Day was celebrated to commemorate the birth anniversary of legendary Hockey Player Major Dhyanchand. Our students of Grade 10 attended the awareness programme on “Entrepreneurship as a Career- Explore an Ocean of Opportunities” organised by Kalpataru Institute of Technology in collaboration with Tumakuru Innovation, Incubation & Entrepreneurship Council and gained knowledge about taking up entrepreneurship as a career.
