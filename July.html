July Month Activities

Doctors’ Day Celebration
1st July – Doctors’ Day was celebrated in the school assembly. Dr. Vivechan, a renowned physician in Tiptur, was the Chief Guest. He addressed the gathering on right ways to sustain and improve one’s health condition.
MT1 Ended
3rd July – MT1 ended for all the students.
Classical Musical Classes
7th July – Classical Musical Classes commenced for interested students. These classes are conducted twice a week by music teacher Smt. Geetha Murali.
Orientation Programme on Assessment & Evaluation
8th July – Orientation Programme on the Assessment and Evaluation Practice was conducted by the principal for parents of Grade 9 & 10 students (3:00–4:00 pm).

PTM for Grades 1 to 8 was held in respective classes (3:50–4:30 pm).
Fire Safety
9th July – A Fire Extinguishing Demo was conducted by Karnataka State Fire & Emergency Services in association with Disaster Management Committee, KCS. High School students performed a mock drill for emergency evacuation.
Guru Purnima Celebration
10th July – Guru Purnima was celebrated with great reverence. Chief Guest was Sri Biligere Krishna Murthy, a renowned writer and retired Principal, Govt. Degree College.
World Population Day
11th July – World Population Day was observed in the school.
Parent Teacher Association Meeting
18th July – PTA meeting was convened at 4 PM in the AV Room with Committee members. Academic and non-academic issues were discussed, and valuable suggestions were shared by parent members.
State-level Debate Competition
21st–22nd July – Selected High School students represented our school in the State-level Debate Competition in Bengaluru, initiated by the Verbattle Foundation.
Heritage Club Programme
23rd July – The Heritage Club organized an orientation for Grade 10 students on History syllabus concepts by Smt. Snigdha Bhaswati, Asst. Professor of History, Govt. First Grade College, Assam. She also interacted with the Social Faculty later.
Kargil Vijay Diwas Celebration
26th July – Kargil Vijay Diwas was celebrated. Chief Guest Havaldar Kattappa Gowda R, a retired Army Officer, motivated students to join the Defence Forces. Cultural programs expressed students’ respect for the nation.
Financial Literacy Programme
31st July – A Financial Literacy programme was organised for Grades 9 & 10 students and teachers. Resource person Mr. Prakash (Retd Deputy Manager, SBI) and Mr. Bhujaga Shayana addressed the gathering on the importance of financial awareness.
