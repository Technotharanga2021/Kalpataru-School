About Us

The Kalpataru Vidya Samsthe(R), Tiptur was established in the year 1962, with a cherished desire of catering to the needs of the learning in and around Tiptur. It is the product of the vision and dynamism of its founding principal Shri. J R Mahalingaiah and the altruistic and munificent donations from the renowned philanthropists like Shri. Pallagatti Adavappa, the founding President of the Kalpataru Vidya Samsthe.

Kalpataru College, which came to light in 1962 with a meager strength of 277 students, went on growing from strength to strength until 1982, when it got bifurcated into Kalpataru Science College and Pallagatti Adavappa Arts and Commerce College, for convenience of administration.

Our Vision

The School strives to create global citizens with deep rooted Indian values so as to make a positive difference in the world they live in.

Our Mission

To provide an outstanding holistic education for our students characterized by successful learning, cultural understanding and emotional well being that provides firm foundation for the next stage of their learning and a safe environment where the art and science unite to produce young people of strong, compassionate character capable of being successful in this competitive world.

Message from the Principal

I derive immense pleasure in talking about our school. We are very proud of the fact that Kalpataru Central School has a spacious building, fully functional laboratories, a well-stocked library with thousands of volumes, a vast playground, and a well-trained, resourceful faculty.

Children enjoy the school environment and, in turn, acquire knowledge and confidently express themselves. Games and co-curricular activities are given due importance in our school.

Our School Stands For:

  • True spirit of learning
  • Inculcating universal human values
  • Showing respect to all faiths
  • Exploring the innovative scientific world
  • Acquiring knowledge to become great individuals

Latest Articles From Activities

Ambedkar Jayanthi

On 14 thApril – Ambedkar Jayanthi was celebrated in the school assembly. Students of Grade 9 and 10 were present on the occasion.

School reopened for the academic year 2025-26

On 26 th May – School reopened for the academic year 2025-26 with a total strength of 1495 students with 776 girls and 719 boys including the new admission. Total Teaching Staff comprises of 68 members with 51 lady teachers and 17 gents teachers and non-teaching staff with 23 ladies and 15 gents. Grade 1 kids were received by the teachers with a pencil as a small token of love for every student.

World Environment Day

On 5th June – World Environment Day was celebrated in the school assembly. Chief Guest was Mr B V Gundappa, An Environmentalist, Lecturer in Biology, C S Pura, Gubbi Taluk. He gave a talk on the conservation of environment. Hon’ble KVS office bearers were present on the occasion.

Forthcoming Diamond Jubilee Celebration

On 19 th June – On account of forthcoming Diamond Jubilee Celebration of Kapataru Vidya Samsthe, Stone – laying ceremony for the new building of the school was held. Mr. Shadakshari, MLA, Tiptur was the Chief Guest. All the KVS Management members were present on the occasion.

International Yoga Day

On 21 st June – International Yoga Day & World Music Day was celebrated in the school assembly. Chief guests were Smt. Chennambika, Sri Kantharaju & Smt Lakshmi, Vivekananda Yoga Training School, Tiptur.
Various Asanas of Yoga were demonstrated by Mr. Rakhesh, Physical Educator and many students of our school. Carnatic Classical music singer Mrs. Kavyasudha, Principal Scientist in a Pharmacuetical Company was the Chief Guest for the Music Day Celebration.
On the same day, Inter-house Song Competition for middle school level was conducted. Theme given was -The Environment. The 1 st place was bagged by Utkarsh House. 2 nd and 3 rd prizes were secured by Samyak and Vriddhi House respectively.

Awareness on Child Safety

On 23 rd June:- An awareness programme on Child Safety Education was conducted. The students from Grade 1 to 5 were shown the videos on Good Touch and Bad Touch.
On the same day, an orientation programme was held on POCSO Act by the principal for the School Bus-Drivers and all the attenders as done every year.

Doctors’ day

On 1 stJuly – Doctors’ day was celebrated in the school assembly. Dr Vivechan, a renowned physician in Tiptur was the Chief Guest. The doctor addressed the gathering on right ways to sustain and improve one’s health condition.

Fire Safety Training

On 9 th July – Fire Extinguishing Demo was initiated by Karnataka State Fire & Emergency Services in association with Disaster Management Committee, KCS in the Kalpataru Grounds. The High School students performed a mock drill for an emergency evacuation in case of any unforeseen disaster.
