About Us

The Kalpataru Vidya Samsthe(R), Tiptur was established in the year 1962, with a cherished desire of catering to the needs of the learning in and around Tiptur. It is the product of the vision and dynamism of its founding principal Shri. J R Mahalingaiah and the altruistic and munificent donations from the renowned philanthropists like Shri. Pallagatti Adavappa, the founding President of the Kalpataru Vidya Samsthe.

Kalpataru College, which came to light in 1962 with a meager strength of 277 students, went on growing from strength to strength until 1982, when it got bifurcated into Kalpataru Science College and Pallagatti Adavappa Arts and Commerce College, for convenience of administration.

Our Vision

The School strives to create global citizens with deep rooted Indian values so as to make a positive difference in the world they live in.

Our Mission

To provide an outstanding holistic education for our students characterized by successful learning, cultural understanding and emotional well being that provides firm foundation for the next stage of their learning and a safe environment where the art and science unite to produce young people of strong, compassionate character capable of being successful in this competitive world.

Latest Articles From Activities

Farewell to the 2024-25 batch

Farewell was organized for the outgoing students of 10th Std on 7th Feb 2025. Smt. Devika B Swamy, Principal, KCS, graced the occasion. The teachers bid adieu with a heavy heart to the outgoing students. On their part, the outgoing students spoke highly of the contribution made by the school to their overall growth. They also promised to perform well in the forthcoming 10th Std CBSE Examination.

Republic Day Celebration

Republic Day was celebrated in our school on 26th January 2025. Sri P K Thipperudrappa, President, KVS hoisted the National Flag all the office bearers of KVS graced the occasion. The most significant aspect of the programme was that the teachers and the students from all the institutions under KVS took part in the programme. Dance programmes and March Past took place on the occasion. It was a huge celebration.

Counselling session for Parents

A counselling session was organized in our school on 7th January 2024. Dr M Sreedhara Murthy, a Renowned Psychologist and well-known counselor was the resource person. Around 100 parents attended the session. It was an interactive session where the parents aired their concerns regarding what the ideal parent behavior should be and how to handle adolescent children. Dr. M Sreedhara Murthy advised the parents not to exert too much pressure on the students. He told them to look up on children endowed with talent and that they have the potential to make things happen in life. He also advised them not to draw compressions among children. Everyone is unique and the parent should have tremendous faith in their children. He gave them tips on how to handle pressure and made the parents realize that an open-ended approach should be adopted by the parents.
